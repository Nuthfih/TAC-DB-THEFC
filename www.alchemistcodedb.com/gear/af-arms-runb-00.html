    Description
    The Envylian mercenary, Lambert, carried this large, roughly forged blade while he travelled the land. It accompanied him through countless battles and is said to be as unbreakable as Lambert's heart.
    Attributes
    Attributes: PATK Up, Slow Up, Hit Rate Up & Chance of Surviving Fatal Dmg Up. Unlocks Master Ability "Mercenary's Pride" when equipped on Lambert (Raises Slash ATK power, AGI & increases chance of surviving fatal Dmg for three turns after appearing on map)
