    Balor's Magical Eye

    Gear Type Accessory
    Rarity 5★
    Item Shard
    Memento
    Basic Information
    Description
    A superb piece of magical gear that allows the wearer to measure the sum of their opponent's magical, physical, and mental prowess, giving their power levels a numerical value. Hawk, companion of Meliodas and friends, often wears this in order to get a read on the group's opponents.
    Attributes
    Attributes: AGI Up, Initial Jewels Up, Hit Rate Up, DEX Up, Enables Master Ability "Hawk Support" when equipped on The Seven Deadly Sins collab units or Lil' Ouroboros if her job type is "Continental Observer" (Recovers own Jewels & raises AGI for three turns)
