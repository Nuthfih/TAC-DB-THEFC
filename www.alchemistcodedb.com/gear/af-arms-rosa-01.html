    Basic Information
    Description
    The beloved scythe of a young woman who is obsessed with red and black. It was forged by a prominent smith, but she has altered the crude original design to suit her unique tastes. She claims that if it is ever destroyed, the Death God who resides within it will be unleashed and reap the souls of all living things.
    Attributes
