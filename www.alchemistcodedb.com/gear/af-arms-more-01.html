    Description
    The mittens of a woman who puts all of her effort into making sure everyone else finds love. She stops just short of kicking those who dare interfere with what she deems to be love's path, focusing all of her attention on couples in whom she sees romantic potential. These mittens are always full of warmth, just like her heart.
