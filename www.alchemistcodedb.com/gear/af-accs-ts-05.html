    Spell Sphere

    Gear Type Accessory
    Rarity 5★
    Item Shard
    Memento
    Basic Information
    Description
    A magic tool designed by Britannia's leading sorcerer. The amazing sphere has concentrated magic that can be unleashed by throwing and shattering it. As it contains various types of magic within it, it is very useful in many situations.
    Attributes
    Attributes: All ATK Up for units of all Elements, Strong vs Gigantic Up, Initial Jewels Up, Enables Master Ability "100% Forced Removal" when equipped on The Seven Deadly Sins collab units (Nullifies Buffs/Debuffs/Quicken/Statuses/Auto Heal/Jewel Auto Charge of target unit for three turns), Activates gear ability when equipped with Demon Sword Lostvayne, War Hammer Gideon, Spirit Spear Chastiefol, Three-Sectioned Staff, Double Bow Harlit, Divine Axe Rhitta, Morning Star Aldan, Elizabeth's Earring, or Zeldris' Sword (Ability varies according to unit)
