    Description
    Ever since I was a little witch, my dream has always been the same: a dream of safety and happiness for the villagers who were always so kind to me...and not just for them, but for a whole world that's kind and gentle--a world where no one has to get hurt. "If my powers can be of use to the people here, then I'll do everything I can for them." It's a dream that has always been as dear as it has been distant. But now that I have comrades who walk the same path alongside me, I've begun to think that this dream may at last become a realty. That is what drives me forward without fear or hesitation, hand in hand with my brother.
