    Description
    One day I was presented with a stuffed animal as thanks, much as one would receive flowers. The sudden gift awakened a memory from long ago within me. It was of my very first time trick or treating. "It's no fun if it's not spooky! That's what Halloween's all about!" Neither my lord's wishes nor mine have changed since that day. I don't think they ever will. The smiles of children bring me joy, but there is another whose smile I wish to see even more. (Julia's present thoughts)
