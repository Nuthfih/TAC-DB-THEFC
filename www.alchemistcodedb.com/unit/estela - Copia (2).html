    Profile

    Estella is a fortune teller from a family of spirit diviners. Though she is highly regarded for her fortune-telling skills, she keeps a certain distance from her clients and avoids getting too deeply involved. However, she sometimes makes an exception and uses her full powers of spirit divination for those whom she sincerely wants to help.
